Graphics Processing Unit (GPU)

The GPU renders images, animations, and video to your display. Unlike CPUs, GPUs use thousands of smaller cores to process massive amounts of visual and AI data simultaneously.

Key GPU Specifications Explained

VRAM (Video Memory)

Dedicated memory on the graphics card (e.g., GDDR6 or GDDR7) used to store high-resolution textures, frame buffers, and 3D assets.

Memory Bus Width

Measured in bits (128-bit, 256-bit, 512-bit), this determines how much data can travel between the GPU core and VRAM at once.

Ray Tracing & Upscaling

Dedicated RT and AI cores simulate realistic lighting and use technologies like DLSS or FSR to boost frame rates at 1440p and 4K.
